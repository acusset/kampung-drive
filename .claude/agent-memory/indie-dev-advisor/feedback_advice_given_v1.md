Advice given, in stated priority order, so I don't repeat myself or contradict past positions:

2. **Replace global waitlist count with estate-level count + shareable referral link** — Robinhood-style progress bar per estate, share-to-WhatsApp as the primary organic growth loop.
3. **Add a concrete dollar figure to the driver hero** — "average Interlace → CBD driver spends $X/month" turns emotional hook into business case.

5. **Rewrite FAQ around real objections** — especially LTA rules (Singapore-specific regulatory concern), payment mechanics, cancellation fears, empty-inbox fear.

Also flagged for deprioritization: further headline iteration, the transit diagram, mock neighbour cards (they read as fake).
