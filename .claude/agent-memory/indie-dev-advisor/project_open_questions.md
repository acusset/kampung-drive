Open questions worth revisiting in future sessions:

1. **Supply-side cold-start.** Drivers are the constrained side. No stated plan yet for how to get the first 5–10 drivers per estate beyond the waitlist form.
2. **LTA / regulatory framing.** Singapore has specific LTA rules about non-commercial carpooling and cost-sharing (Grab/Ryde had to navigate these). Not addressed on landing page or in conversation yet — could be a real product/legal constraint, not just an FAQ item.
3. **Monetization.** No pricing model discussed. Landing page micro-copy says "Free while we're building" — implies future monetization but no hypothesis on take-rate, subscription, or B2B (condo management partnerships?) angle.
4. **Distribution beyond waitlist.** No stated channel for driving initial traffic to the landing page. Condo WhatsApp groups? Facebook estate groups? Referral loop not yet built in.
5. **Retention / activation loop.** What brings a matched pair back week 2? Habit is the commute itself, but the app's role in the recurring habit is unclear.

**Why:** These are the fundamentals that determine whether the product can work — worth surfacing whenever Antoine shifts from copy/UI questions to product/strategy questions.

**How to apply:** If a conversation drifts into feature-brainstorming without touching these, gently redirect. Related: [[project-kampung-ride-core]].
